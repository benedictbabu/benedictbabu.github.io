// 04. Background
Reliable firmware from bare metal to edge connectivity.

I am Benedict Babu, an Embedded Firmware Engineer and IoT Consultant. My work centers on building maintainable low-level software that performs reliably in the field—whether optimizing sleep states down to single-digit microamps, crafting clean RTOS architectures, or unblocking tricky hardware bring-ups.

Beyond commercial consulting for startups and hardware companies, I dedicate time to consulting with student teams, academic researchers, and makers on proof-of-concept builds, schematic validation, and test automation.
